How It Works

  1. 1

    Add your subjects — enter each one with a difficulty level and the total hours you need this week.

  2. 2

    Set your schedule — pick the daily time window, your peak energy hours, and which days you're available.

  3. 3

    Generate — hit the button and the AI solver builds an optimised timetable, placing hard subjects in your peak slots.

  4. 4

    Fine-tune & export — drag blocks to reschedule, tap cells to block busy slots, then export as a PDF.
